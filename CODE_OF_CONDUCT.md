# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the code, not
about the person writing it.

## What that means here

Opusline is a small, single-maintainer project. There is no committee — the
maintainer reads everything and decides. That makes the bar simple:

- **Welcome**: questions that seem obvious, bug reports without a reproduction,
  disagreement with a design decision, "I don't understand why this works this
  way", French, English, or a mix of both.
- **Not welcome**: personal attacks, harassment, demeaning or exclusionary
  language, unwanted personal attention, publishing someone's private
  information, and treating a volunteer's time as something you have bought.

Nobody here owes you a review, a fix, or a feature. Nobody here gets to be
unpleasant about that either.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests, in discussions, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer at theo.benoit16@gmail.com. Reports stay private. The
maintainer will act on what is reported — an edit, a lock, a block — and will
say what was done to the person who reported it.

If the problem is with the maintainer, say so in the same place; there is no
better address, and the project would rather hear it than not.
